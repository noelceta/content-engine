## Our angle

The guide for someone who just bought their first burr grinder, written by someone who takes theirs apart regularly: real photos of each step on an actual home grinder, a straight answer on rice vs tablets with what the manufacturers actually say side by side, and the step every top page skips, which is getting your grind setting back after you put it together again.

Why someone picks ours over the top 10:
- **Brand pages** (Breville, Fellow) only cover their own gear and end in a sales pitch. Fellow's frequency advice contradicts itself (intro says monthly, its own table says 1 to 3 months).
- **Nobody** tells you to write down or photograph your grind setting before pulling the burr, or how to re-dial afterwards. Breville even lists "difficulty adjusting grind settings" as a symptom and then doesn't help.
- **Rice** gets asserted ("don't" or "fine for blade") without showing the actual manufacturer positions together.
- **Videos/social** show it but don't give you a checklist you can follow with the grinder in pieces on the counter.

## Facts we'll use

**Breville** (https://www.breville.com/us/en/blog/coffee-and-espresso/how-to-clean-a-coffee-grinder.html)
- Blade grinders "should ideally be cleaned weekly." Burr grinders "perform best when cleaned monthly." Also: "There are no hard and fast rules." Darker roasts need more frequent cleaning.
- Signs it needs cleaning: visible residue or oil, off-color grinds after switching beans, unusual noises or slower grinding, difficulty adjusting grind settings.
- Rice: blade grinders only, "for one minute." On burr grinders it "can damage the machinery and may void the warranty."
- "Never submerge an electrical appliance." Parts are "sensitive to rust." Avoid warm soapy water and machine washing.
- After reassembly, run a small batch of beans to "season" the burrs (no amount given).
- Blade steps: unplug, brush or compressed air, wipe blades and chamber with a damp cloth, dry fully.

**Fellow** (https://fellowproducts.com/blogs/learn/how-to-clean-a-coffee-grinder)
- Deep clean "every 1–3 months, depending on use." Table: occasional use every 2 to 3 months; daily drinker every 1 to 2 months; espresso, dark or oily beans "once or twice per month"; stalling or off taste: "Clean immediately."
- No water near burrs, motor or internal parts "unless the manufacturer explicitly says it is safe." No metal brushes or abrasive scourers on burrs.
- Tools: stiff-bristled brush (toothbrush works), microfiber cloth, dry towel, optional small vacuum or silicone blower.
- Recheck before using (the fetch only summarized these, not verbatim): tablet amount "small handful", purge "up to 1 lb", FAQ saying to run coffee through a new grinder, cleaning vs descaling.

**OXO Brew Conical Burr manual, model 8717000** (https://www.williams-sonoma.com/netstorage/pdf/OXO-Brew-Conical-Burr-Coffee-Grinder-8717000-UserManual.pdf)
- p.10: Run 10 seconds with an empty hopper, then unplug. Remove hopper: turn knob counterclockwise until it clicks ("trap door" keeps beans in). Remove upper burr: lift the metal handle, twist counterclockwise, pull up. "Only the upper burr is removable."
- p.10: Clean burrs and chute with a small bristle brush. "Never use water or other liquids to clean the inside of the Grinder/burrs."
- p.10: Replace burr: align indents with tabs, turn clockwise until it clicks, pull the handle to test. Beans left around the burrs can stop it locking.
- p.11: Hand wash hopper and grounds container, rinse and dry thoroughly. Scoop is dishwasher safe. "Do not put any part of your Grinder in the dishwasher." No abrasive pads, harsh chemicals or spray cleaners. "We recommend grinding only coffee in this Grinder."
- p.12: "Dark, oily beans can clog grounds chute." Fix: brush the chute; if it persists, use coffee grinder cleaning tablets.
- French section p.16 says new units may have a small amount of grounds inside from factory quality testing. Find the matching English line before using.

**Urnex Grindz** (https://urnex.com/grindz-grinder-cleaning-tablets)
- Dose: espresso grinders "1 jar cap (35-40 g)", shop grinders "2 caps (70-80 g)".
- Directions: empty the grinder, add Grindz, set to medium and grind, then repeat with coffee beans to purge.
- "Do not use in superautomatic machine grinders."
- "All ingredients are food safe." Made from "natural, biodegradable and gluten free ingredients." No frequency given.
- $25.99 for a 430 g jar (as of 2026-11-10).
