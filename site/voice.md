# How I write

Paste 2 or 3 of your best pieces of writing below (posts, emails, anything that sounds like you). Claude copies the rhythm, not the content.

- First person, casual, like talking to a friend.
- I say what I'd actually do, and what I wouldn't bother with.
- Short sentences. Some one-line paragraphs. No corporate words.
- I admit when something is a matter of opinion.
